# Site topology

## Home

1. Full-viewport atmosphere: dense star field, four-column precision grid and moving guide markers.
2. Procedural WebGL globe: night-side terrain, city-light clusters, atmospheric rim, slow axial rotation and pointer parallax.
3. Left backlight and three thin orbital ellipses provide the eclipse composition seen in the supplied reference.
4. Fixed chrome: menu and language controls at the upper guide intersections; motto, mark, copyright and welcome label at the bottom.
5. Hero copy at 52.19% / 41.23% of the desktop viewport; constellation system at the upper right; bracketed work link beneath the globe.
6. Full-screen navigation retains the same vertical grid and transition language used by the contact route.

## Contact

Reference: `https://claudiuangheloni.com/contact.html`, inspected at 1363 × 936.

1. Fixed atmospheric layer: dark gradient, star canvas, four-column guide grid.
2. Fixed chrome: menu trigger at the first guide, language switcher at the last guide, scroll indicator at the far right.
3. Page grid: 220 px left rail, responsive gap, fluid contact stage.
4. Rail: two vertical display words and three contact-detail lines.
5. Contact stage: eyebrow line, four-line display headline, low-opacity watermark, five form rows, note and segmented-border submit button.
6. Deferred footer chrome: motto, center mark/legal copy, right-aligned social links, revealed at the bottom or while the menu is open.
7. Full-screen menu: background replaces page content while preserving grid, fixed trigger and footer chrome.

Desktop page measurements use 12.5% left/right insets within a maximum 1440 px shell. The mobile breakpoint is approximately 992 px; the layout becomes a single column and the rail title becomes horizontal.
